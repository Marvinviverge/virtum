# Code of conduct

**English** | [Français](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fr.md)

Virtum is a small project, maintained by one person and open to everyone. This code applies to every space of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any exchange about Virtum.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Criticize code, ideas and decisions, never people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful: a minimal example says more than a long debate.
- Accept that a proposal can be declined. The maintainer explains why.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, intimidation, or personal or political attacks.
- Comments that demean someone for their identity, origin, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, appearance, religion or level of experience.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeated spam or self-promotion unrelated to Virtum.
- Insisting after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behavior that breaks this code, write to **contact@virtum.dev**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so in the message: it will be handled with the same care, and you can also contact GitHub through its [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Security vulnerabilities do not go through this channel: see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Consequences

Depending on the severity, the maintainer may edit or delete a message, give a warning, or temporarily or permanently block someone from the project. The decision is explained to the person concerned, unless that would put the person who reported it at risk.
